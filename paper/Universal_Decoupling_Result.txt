Status date: 9 September 2026


1. THE EXPLICIT GRAPH

Let Gamma have underlying set Z_12 x Z_5 and multiplication

    (e,i)(f,j) = (e+f mod 12, i + 2^e j mod 5).

This is well defined because 2^12 = 1 modulo 5.  Let

    S = {(1,0), (11,0), (5,1), (7,2), (6,0), (6,3)}.

The set S is inverse closed and does not contain the identity.  Define G_60
to be the undirected Cayley graph Cay(Gamma,S): its vertices are the 60
elements of Gamma, and x is adjacent to xs for every x in Gamma and s in S.
Under the integer label

    label(e,i) = 5e+i,

the identity is vertex 0 and

    N(0) = {5,26,30,33,37,55}.

This definition gives a finite simple, vertex-transitive, 6-regular graph
with 60 vertices and 180 edges.  The frozen edge-list file has SHA-256

    85914250312a4fe8d1a67d747fea2ee1772c9762796af90095efa61fb4c0400c.


2. THE BALANCED-PUNCTURE CERTIFICATE

The decisive finite statement is the following.

Balanced-puncture lemma.  The graph G_60-0 has a proper 3-colouring.  In
every proper 3-colouring of G_60-0, each of the three colours occurs exactly
twice on N(0).

For a transparent normal form, the surviving edge 1-6 can be assigned
colours 0 and 1.  Exhaustive enumeration then gives exactly 16 normalized
colourings, or 96 colour-labelled colourings before quotienting by the six
global colour permutations.  In every normalized colouring,

    (c(5),c(26),c(30),c(33),c(37),c(55)) = (1,1,0,2,2,0).

The Lean source/certificate package encodes one positive colouring, eighteen
search-tree certificates excluding a unique occurrence of a specified
colour on the punctured neighbourhood, and three search-tree certificates
excluding a missing colour.  Together these imply the exact 2+2+2 split.


3. WHY THE CERTIFICATE PROVES THE k=4 RESULT

First, a proper 3-colouring of G_60-0 can be extended by giving vertex 0 a
fresh fourth colour, so chi(G_60) is at most 4.  If G_60 itself had a proper
3-colouring, its restriction to G_60-0 would be one of the colourings in the
balanced-puncture lemma.  Whichever colour were assigned to 0 already occurs
on two neighbours of 0.  This is impossible in a proper colouring.  Hence

    chi(G_60) = 4.

Second, left multiplication in Gamma acts transitively by graph
automorphisms.  It transports the displayed colouring of G_60-0 to a proper
3-colouring of G_60-v for every vertex v.  No G_60-v can be 2-colourable,
because giving v a third colour would then 3-colour G_60.  Therefore

    chi(G_60-v) = 3 for every vertex v.

Third, suppose that deleting an edge uv produced a proper 3-colouring.
Translate u to 0 by a left multiplication, and let s be the translated image
of v.  Restrict this supposed colouring of G_60-0s to G_60-0.  The colour
assigned to 0 occurs on exactly two neighbours of 0.  Deleting 0s can remove
at most one of the two resulting conflicts, so the other incident edge is
still monochromatic.  This contradiction proves

    chi(G_60-e) = 4 for every edge e.

Consequently G_60 is 4-vertex-critical and has no critical edge.  It is an
explicit solution of Dirac's k=4 existence problem, subject to the
verification boundary in Section 6 below.


4. THE CORRECT UNIVERSAL STATEMENT

For an integer k at least 2, define D(k) to mean:

    There exists a finite simple graph H such that

        chi(H) = k,
        chi(H-v) = k-1 for every vertex v, and
        chi(H-e) = k for every edge e.

The corrected Universal Decoupling Theorem is the existential classification

    For every integer k >= 2, D(k) holds if and only if k >= 4.

Here "immune to edge deletion" means immune to deletion of every individual
edge.  It cannot mean immunity to all sets of edges: deleting the entire edge
set lowers the chromatic number of every graph with chromatic number at least
2 to 1.


5. PROOF OF THE EXISTENTIAL CLASSIFICATION

Necessity for k=2.  A 2-chromatic graph contains an edge uv.  If a
2-vertex-critical graph containing uv had a third vertex w, then deleting w
would leave the edge uv and hence a graph of chromatic number at least 2.
That contradicts vertex-criticality.  The graph must therefore be K_2, and
its unique edge is critical.  Thus D(2) is false.

Necessity for k=3.  Let H be 3-vertex-critical.  Since H is not bipartite,
it contains an odd cycle C.  No vertex lies outside C, because deleting such
a vertex would leave the 3-chromatic subgraph C.  The cycle C has no chord:
a chord divides an odd cycle into two cycles of opposite parity, one of which
is a proper odd cycle, and deletion of a vertex omitted by that proper cycle
would again leave a 3-chromatic graph.  Hence H is precisely an odd cycle.
Deleting any edge turns it into a path, so every edge is critical.  Thus D(3)
is false.

Sufficiency for k=4 is the graph G_60 proved above.

Sufficiency for every k>=5 is Jensen's 2002 theorem: for every such k there
exists a k-vertex-critical graph without a critical edge.  It also follows
from the stronger theorem of Skottova and Steiner that (k,r)-graphs exist for
every k>=5 and r>=1, by setting r=1.

Combining these four cases proves D(k) if and only if k>=4 for k>=2.

This is not a pointwise characterization of graphs.  The formula

    H is vertex-critical and edge-immune  iff  chi(H) >= 4

is false: K_4 is vertex-critical and 4-chromatic, but every edge of K_4 is
critical.  The words "there exists" in D(k) are indispensable.  If the
classification is instead quantified over every natural number k, D(k)
should additionally require E(H) to be nonempty.  Otherwise K_1, and under
the usual conventions the null graph, create vacuous low-chromatic
exceptions.


6. VERIFICATION AND TRUST BOUNDARY

The graph and the balanced-puncture property have been checked by several
independently written exact programs.  Separate finite-domain, CNF/DPLL,
odd-cycle-transversal, and MILP formulations agree.  The graph-agnostic
audits directly check G_60, all 60 vertex deletions, and all 180 edge
deletions; the compact proof additionally checks the complete punctured
colouring space and the Cayley translations.

A Lean 4 source/certificate package formalizes the group law, edge relation,
punctured colourings, exhaustive balanced-puncture certificates, and symmetry
transport. On 9 September 2026 both complementary projects completed external
builds with Lean 4.19.0 and Mathlib v4.19.0. The compact executable reported
180 edges, every concrete vertex witness valid, and three-colour defect 2.
The structured theorem

    Dirac60.order60_solution :
      FourVertexCritical G and EdgeImmuneAtFour G

compiled successfully. Its axiom report was

    [propext, Classical.choice, Lean.ofReduceBool, Quot.sound]

with no sorryAx. The independent structured replay passed all 21 cases and
all 35,967 nodes, and its corrected SHA-256 manifest passed in full. Thus the
order-60 k=4 statement is Lean-checked within the disclosed native-code
evaluation trust boundary represented by Lean.ofReduceBool.

Even after these builds, the whole Universal Decoupling Theorem is not thereby
formalized end to end in Lean.  The new k=4 implication would be
Lean-checked, and the k=2 and k=3 arguments are elementary, but the k>=5
implication still invokes Jensen's published theorem.  A fully Lean-verified
universal theorem requires a formalization of Jensen's general construction
and proof.  Merely declaring Jensen's result as a Lean axiom does not provide
kernel-checked evidence for that imported mathematical claim.

Finally, this result has not yet undergone independent public reproduction or
peer review.  Those steps remain necessary before presenting it as an
accepted resolution in the literature.
